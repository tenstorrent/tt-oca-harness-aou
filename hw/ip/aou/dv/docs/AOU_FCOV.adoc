[[aou-dv-functional-coverage]]
== AoU Functional Coverage Plan

=== Summary

Functional coverage shows which legal behaviours of the AoU bridge the
regression actually drove: every flit and handshake on the UCIe FDI, every
link-state and activation transition, every message type and where it sits in
a flit, every way credit is granted and used, every AXI request shape and
attribute at every port, and every RP width pairing. It doesn't judge the
DUT; the always-on checkers do. A hit bin means the situation occurred in a
passing run, so the checkers looked at it.

The model has 33 testbench covergroups and a generic AXI4 monitor bound to
every S and M port. The full regression of the model as it stood at PR #142
(11 configurations, VCS, 5566 passing T1 runs merged) hit 4447 of the 4479
headline bins (99.3%); since then the AXI4 back-pressure group has grown (AW, W
and AR stall lengths, both outcomes of a stall at the limit) and the two
asymmetric-RP-mapping groups were added:

[cols="3,1,1",options="header"]
|===
| Part | Bins hit | %
| AXI4 port monitors (section 9), the generic AXI view of every port | 2781/2800 | 99.3
| AoU-specific groups (sections 1-8) | 1666/1679 | 99.2
| Apart from the headline: tagged `v0.8` (need a v0.8 DUT or partner) | 0/7 | -
| Apart from the headline: tagged `v0.9draft` (shared Activation-flit layouts) | 8/8 | -
|===

`make fc_report` prints the live number for a set of runs; these figures are
only a snapshot.

Where the real holes are (details in <<aou-fcov-gaps>>):

* *The v0.8 behaviour the RTL doesn't implement yet:* the ERROR activation
  state.

Several behaviours have no bins at all: registers, interrupts, QoS arbitration
outcomes, the DUT's own splitter and aggregator, resets during traffic, and
interactions between link events and traffic in flight (<<aou-fcov-not-covered>>).

[[aou-fcov-reading]]
=== How to Read the Coverage

* *Counted:* a bin counts when any passing run of any configuration hit it.
  The bench's coverage service counts, not the simulator's covergroup engine,
  so VCS and Verilator agree bin for bin. Failing and expected-fail runs never
  count.
* *Ignored and illegal:* an _ignored_ value or combination can't happen as
  described, or isn't a target (for example stimulus the plan leaves out
  because the spec doesn't define it). It isn't counted. An _illegal_ value is
  a protocol violation. It isn't counted either, and a hit is reported by the
  checker that owns that rule.
* *Tags:* `v0.8` marks behaviour AoU v0.8 defines that the RTL doesn't
  implement yet; `v0.9draft` marks a layout only the v0.9 draft of the AoU
  flit rules (4.4) allows. Bins with a revision tag are left out of the
  headline figure until the DUT targets that revision, and the reports total
  them apart. `rtl-limit` marks a legal case this RTL never produces by design
  (the DUT's WriteResp packing density); those bins are also left out of the
  headline and totalled apart, so a hit shows if the RTL changes, and the tag
  is removed then. `rare` marks stimulus that is deliberately infrequent; those
  bins stay in the headline and are also totalled apart.
* *Direction:* _tx/rx_ is seen from the remote-die model (tx = model to DUT,
  rx = DUT to model). _l2r/r2l_ is the AXI path: a local manager through the
  DUT to the remote die, or the reverse.
* *Report names:* `summary.txt` and `holes.txt` print a group as
  `<layer>.<group>` (AXI4 monitors: `axi4.<port>.<group>`), the last column of
  each table. The covergroup in the code has the same name.
* *Whose choice:* groups marked *(stimulus)* record what the bench did; a
  hole there needs stimulus. Groups or bins marked *(DUT)* record a choice the
  DUT made; a hole there may need different RTL, not more stimulus.

The AoU specification v0.8 is the deciding reference; UCIe r1.1 chapter 8
defines the FDI, AMBA AXI4 the ports, and the MAS and the integration guide
the DUT's own features.

=== Coverage Groups

[[aou-fcov-fdi]]
==== 1. FDI Mainband (UCIe r1.1 chapter 8)

The flit stream between the DUT's protocol layer and the Adapter, sampled by
the L1 monitor per flit and per handshake.
Code: `uvm/env/fdi/l1/aou_fdi_cov.svh`.

[cols="2,3,5,2",options="header"]
|===
| Group | What it shows | Bins | Report name
| `cg_fdi_xfer` | Every flit, each way, at every FDI width | direction × beat {32, 64, 128 B}; cycles stalled inside the flit {0, 1, 2-4, 5+} (`pl_trdy` low on TX, bubbles on RX) and idle cycles before it {0, 1-3, 4+}, each × direction | `l1.cg_fdi_xfer`
| `cg_fdi_cancel` | `pl_flit_cancel` handling | first or second 128 B half × with or without `pl_valid` in the cancel cycle; 1st, 2nd, 3rd+ cancel of one flit; TX stalled or not | `l1.cg_fdi_cancel`
| `cg_fdi_stall` (DUT) | The stall handshake before a state change (UCIe r1.1 8.3.2) | `pl_stallreq` to `lp_stallack` latency {2, 3, 4-6, 7+ cycles} × request at a flit boundary or mid-flit; `lp_stallack` hold {1, 2-4, 5+}; `pl_trdy` high or low when the request rose, × boundary or mid-flit | `l1.cg_fdi_stall`
| `cg_fdi_abandon` | A partial flit dropped because the Adapter left Active mid-flit | TX, RX | `l1.cg_fdi_abandon`
|===

Excluded from `cg_fdi_stall`: an `lp_stallack` held one cycle, which can't
happen (it may not fall while `pl_stallreq` is high, and `pl_stallreq` falls
only after it has seen the acknowledge, so the acknowledge lasts at least 2
cycles); `pl_trdy` low at a flit-boundary request, which the monitor never
records (it records `pl_trdy` low at the request only for a request inside a
flit, the UCIe r1.1 8.3.2 corner the bin is there for); and the latencies the
DUT never chooses, since it acknowledges a request at a flit boundary in 2
cycles and one inside a flit in 3 or more (the acknowledge can't rise inside a
flit): boundary × 3, 4-6 and 7+ cycles, and mid-flit × 2 cycles. The regression
of 2026-11-04 (5610 passing runs) saw 106869 boundary requests, all answered
in 2 cycles, and 148294 mid-flit ones, all in 3 or more.

==== 2. FDI Link State and Bring-up (UCIe r1.1 8.2 and 8.3)

Every change of `pl_state_sts` or of the DUT's `lp_state_req`, and the DUT's
bring-up controller (a DUT feature the MAS and the integration guide
describe) as the bench's reference model of it tracks it.
Code: `uvm/env/fdi/l2/aou_lsm_cov.svh`, bins in `aou_lsm_fc_bins.svh`.

[cols="2,3,5,2",options="header"]
|===
| Group | What it shows | Bins | Report name
| `cg_link_state` | The Adapter states the DUT lives through, and what it requests in each | Transitions: Reset→Active; Active↔Retrain; Active→L1; L1→Retrain; Active→LinkReset→Reset; Active→Disabled→Reset; Active, Retrain or Reset→LinkError; LinkError→Reset. The 7 states. DUT requests NOP, Active, L1. Status × request: only the pairs UCIe r1.1 Table 8-3 gives a meaning to (Reset: NOP, Active; Active: Active; L1: L1, Active; Retrain: NOP, Active; LinkError, LinkReset, Disabled: NOP) | `l2.cg_link_state`
| `cg_bringup_fsm` | The bring-up controller's sequence, in FDI terms | waiting for `pl_inband_pres` and giving up when it drops; the sideband wake handshake; the Active request and Active; Retrain and its return or link error; L1 and its two exits (clock wake, or a wake request); LinkError or link down back to Reset (17 transitions) | `l2.cg_bringup_fsm`
|===

Excluded: Active straight to Reset in both groups. The UCIe r1.1 Adapter
state diagram (8.3.3, Figure 8-17) reaches Reset from LinkReset, Disabled or
LinkError; only a hard reset without warning while Active gets there, which is
no normal scenario, and resets in traffic stay covered through LinkReset and
Disabled. Requests for Retrain, LinkReset or Disabled, which this DUT doesn't
make (UCIe r1.1 8.3.3.4: a protocol layer requests Retrain only in Raw Format;
LinkReset and Disabled are software's, on the UCIe controller). A reserved
request is illegal.

The remote-die model's activation state machine, which follows the DUT's
handshake, and every Activation message sent or received.
Code: `uvm/env/l3/aou_l3_cov.svh`, `uvm/env/aou_t1_status.svh`.

[cols="2,3,5,2",options="header"]
|===
| Group | What it shows | Bins | Report name
| `cg_act_fsm` | Every activation-state transition, including losing the link mid-handshake | DISABLED→ACTIVATE→ENABLED→DEACTIVATE→DISABLED; back to DISABLED from ACTIVATE, ENABLED or DEACTIVATE when the FDI link is lost (LinkReset, link down); ACTIVATE, ENABLED or DEACTIVATE→ERROR and ERROR→DISABLED (`v0.8`, the non-orderly shutdown) | `l3.cg_act_fsm`
| `cg_act_op` | Every Activation message by op, direction and legality in the current state | {ActivateReq, ActivateAck, DeactivateReq, DeactivateAck, reserved op} × tx/rx × legal/illegal | `l3.cg_act_op`
| `cg_act_flit` | What shares a flit with an Activation message | ActivateReq or ActivateAck alone or with one CrdtGrant; DeactivateReq alone or with responses; DeactivateAck alone; × tx/rx. AoU v0.8 4.4 puts an Activation message alone in its flit; the shared layouts follow the v0.9 draft of 4.4 and are tagged `v0.9draft`, so they stay out of the headline figure | `l3.cg_act_flit`
| `cg_stray_dack` (DUT) | An out-of-protocol DeactivateAck in ENABLED reached an accepted ending | one bin: ignored, flagged on arrival, or an error at the next deactivation. Any of them is acceptable and one DUT shows one or two, so the target is that one was reached; the run log names which | `env.cg_stray_dack`
|===

Excluded: a reserved op (AoU v0.8 Table 27, ops 4-15), which no compliant

fails the run, so it can't occur in a passing run (illegal ops come only from
the bench's injection); a DUT ActivateAck without its initial CrdtGrant and a
DUT ActivateReq carrying one (this DUT always sends the grant in its Ack's
flit).

==== 5. Credits, Resource Planes and RP Mapping (AoU v0.8 chapter 6; MAS 3.10)

Credit is per RP and per class (WriteReq, ReadReq, WriteData, ReadData,
WriteResp); resource planes are independent. The asymmetric RP mapping
(`ASYMMETRIC_RP_PROC_CON`, MAS 3.10.4) routes each S port's requests to a TX
lane and may merge several ports onto one; the bench programs a random legal
mapping per seed, and the two `cg_rp_map` groups show which routes and
mappings carried traffic.
Code: `uvm/env/l3/aou_l3_cov.svh`, `uvm/env/aou_t1_crd_flow_chk.svh`,
`uvm/env/aou_t1_crd_rr_chk.svh`, `uvm/env/aou_t1_rp_map_chk.svh`.

[cols="2,3,5,2",options="header"]
|===
| Group | What it shows | Bins | Report name
| `cg_credit` | Every way credit is granted, used and run out of, each way | class × {credit returned in the flit header; in a CrdtGrant message; both in one flit; a credited message sent} × tx/rx. Model waits on zero DUT credit (tx). DUT sends as soon as a grant ends its zero credit (rx). Model advertises more than its buffer holds, which the DUT must absorb (tx). Model advertises credit for the request-direction classes (WriteReq, ReadReq, WriteData) after its DeactivateReq and DeactivateAck, which may reach a DUT already DISABLED (tx) | `l3.cg_credit`
| `cg_crd` | Resource-plane independence on the DUT's TX | per class: an RP with work and no credit (starved), and an RP that sent with credit while another RP was starved | `env.cg_crd`
| `cg_crd_rr` | The DUT's round-robin return of header credit over RPs (MAS 3.9) | RPs owed credit when a test opened a window {2, 3, 4} | `env.cg_crd_rr`
| `cg_rp_map_req` | The route each request the DUT sent took (MAS 3.10.4) | write/read; the S port it came from × the TX lane it went on (every port to every lane); route: mapping disabled, enabled on the port's own lane, moved to another port's lane alone, merged with other ports; route × direction | `env.cg_rp_map_req`
| `cg_rp_map_cfg` | Each mapping that carried a request, once per programming or activation | shape: disabled with the lane selects at reset or not, enabled as the identity, moved ports only, one merge group of 2, 3 or 4 ports, two pairs; the DUT's RP count against the remote die's (2-4 × 1-4); merged or not, crossed with that pairing; a merge group of one width or of mixed widths; a port on a lane whose own port is narrower | `env.cg_rp_map_cfg`
|===

Excluded: the model's own credit counter saturating (no target); the model
sending without credit, an injection AoU v0.8 6.1 gives no receiver reaction
for, so the plan doesn't run it as a target; a round-robin window with fewer
than two RPs owed, which has nothing to order; the width points of

every pairing bin is reachable on every configuration (a 2-RP build has no
merge of 3); the target is the union over configurations. Illegal: the DUT
sending without credit (a checker failure).

[[aou-fcov-not-covered]]
==== 10. Not Covered by Functional Bins

These are verified by checks and directed tests and measured by code coverage,
with no functional group: register fields and access (the register bench has
no covergroups); interrupt sources, masking and W1C, beyond the watchdog's;
QoS arbitration outcomes (`attr_cg` only shows that AxQOS values cross the
link), including the arbitration inside a merge group of the asymmetric RP
mapping; DEST_RP permutations (the asymmetric mapping has `cg_rp_map_req`
and `cg_rp_map_cfg`); the DUT's own AXI splitter (`split_cg` is the
bench's) and aggregator; software and core resets during traffic; and
performance, which the performance tests check against their own targets.
Groups for them are open work in the verification plan's road-map.

[[aou-fcov-gaps]]
=== Gaps Against the Specification

Spec behaviours with no bin, or holes that point at a real gap. None of them
has a bin added yet; each is recorded in the verification plan's road-map
(coverage closure, deferred) for follow-up.

. *DUT-initiated L1 entry.* The DUT has no way to start PM entry: no register
  or pin asks it to, and it requests L1 only after the Adapter reports L1
  (integration guide 3.15 and 6.6, MAS 7.3.6, where PM entry is software's on
  the UCIe controller after a deactivation). The passing runs show 527 L1
  entries, every one the Adapter's, with the DUT's L1 request one cycle later.
  `cg_link_state` status Active × request L1 is therefore ignored. UCIe r1.1
  8.2.9 has the protocol layer start PM entry, so a software-controlled L1
  request is proposed as an enhancement; the bin returns if the DUT gains one.
. *Which ReadData answers a ReadReq.* AoU v0.8 adds a rule for the DLENGTH
  of the ReadData that answers a ReadReq. `cg_msg` counts ReadData sizes and
  `width_cg` counts ARSIZE, but nothing crosses a request's size with the size
  of its answer.
. *Traffic in DEACTIVATE (AoU v0.8 8.4).* No bin covers responses sent after a
  side's own DeactivateReq, or who deactivated first (DUT, remote, both). Only
  same-flit responses (`cg_act_flit`) and late credit (`cg_credit`) are
  covered. Activation order (DUT first, remote first, simultaneous) isn't
  covered either.
. *ERROR from DISABLED.* v0.8 enters ERROR on any inconsistent request or
  acknowledge, for example an ActivateAck in DISABLED. The `v0.8` bins start
  only from ACTIVATE, ENABLED and DEACTIVATE.
. *DUT WriteResp density (DUT).* `cg_wresp_flit` rx 16-47 and 48 WriteResps
  per flit are cases the DUT can't produce, tagged `rtl-limit`. Its TX
  packer (as the RTL shows; the MAS doesn't say) takes at most one WriteResp
  into its ring for each FDI chunk it sends, and the ring holds about 105
  granules, so one flit carries at most about 12 WriteResps (6 with 128 B
  chunks; the 256 B FDI's one-step packer fills a whole flit from one cycle's
  pops, so no more there); the most measured under heavy two-way traffic with
  bursty `pl_trdy` is 11. The model's side (tx) is covered:
  the remote die returns WriteResps in batches on about one seed in eight.
. *Link events with traffic in flight.* No bin combines a link event
  (Retrain, LinkError, L1, reset) with traffic in flight or the activation
  state. This is the riskiest interaction and only directed tests cover it.
. *Profiles and FLEX/USER fields (AoU v0.8).* `cg_act_profile` counts the
  profile fields exchanged in the ActivateReq and `cg_flex` the FLEX values of
  the AXI messages; no bin crosses FLEX with the message type or the port.
. *Credit encodings.* No bin records which credit encodings (0, 1, 4, ..., 128
  per class) were sent or received; the credit accounting checks are the only
  evidence that each one decodes correctly.

Each bin also names the plan items it is evidence for, in its `plan_ids`
field in `fc.json` and `fc_merged.json` (a group's `plan_ids` are the items
all its bins serve). The bench's groups set them where they are declared,
with `aou_fc_group::plan()` calls (every bin of a group, of a coverpoint or
cross, or the bins matching a pattern); the AXI4 monitors from the `FC_PLAN`
table next to their bins, the same for every port. An ID is the text of a
surviving item (`4.9`, not a merged `4.10`), and `make check-config` checks
every one against `docs/test_plan_items.yaml`. Given the coverage
(`scripts/plan_status.py --fc <fc_merged.json>`, which a coverage regression
passes its union), the plan's status page lists each stimulus-shape item with
its bins hit over total, per coverpoint, and its holes. Groups no plan item
names: `cg_stray_dack` (out-of-protocol messages have no plan item),
`split_cg` (the remote model's splitter, not the DUT's), and the AXI4 `cg_cmd`.

=== Goals and Closure

[cols="2,1,4",options="header"]
|===
| Metric | Target | Scope
| Functional coverage, every testbench covergroup | 100% of the targets, crosses included | The union of passing T1 runs over every configuration (`aou_sim_cfg.toml` targets)
| AXI4 port monitors | 100% | Per port (`axi_tx_rp<n>`, `axi_rx_rp<n>`), each the union of every configuration
| Code coverage of `RTL/`: line, condition, branch | 100% of what the configuration instantiates | Per configuration and on the union, T1 and register bench runs merged; the integrator-replaced library cells excluded
| FSM coverage | Every state and every transition reachable without a reset | Reset-only transitions excluded
| Toggle | Reviewed, not a gate | Triage at the end of closure
| Rule inventory | Every rule hit in a passing run; every negative rule fired in a negative test | `scripts/rule_inventory.py`, `docs/checker_inventory.md`
|===

* *Nothing is waived.* A bin that is not a target (unreachable, undefined
  stimulus, not meaningful for this DUT) is changed in its bins table to
  ignored or illegal, with the reason beside it; the change is reviewed as a
  change to the coverage model, and this document changes with it. Until then
  it stays a hole.
* A `v0.8` or `v0.9draft` bin is out of the headline figure and reported
  apart; it joins the headline when the DUT targets that revision.
* A bin behind an open DUT bug is closed by the fix, or by a variant that
  avoids the bug, never by removing the bin.
* The owner's coverage decisions are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` D10; the open
  closure work is in the verification plan's road-map.

=== Details

The rest of this chapter is reference material for people changing the
coverage model or its tooling.

==== Collection and Reports

* Covergroups live in the layer models, the T1 env and the bound AXI4
  monitors. Each covergroup and the coverage service (`cov/sv/aou_fc_pkg.sv`)
  take their bins from one table macro in a `*_fc_bins.svh` file next to the
  covergroup (through `cov/sv/aou_fc_macros.svh`), so the two can't drift. The
  service's count is the number: Verilator compiles covergroups but scores them
  as zero, and the commercial simulators weight coverpoints and crosses their
  own way. Cross exclusions and tags are the service's alone.
* A table entry is a value or range bin, an ignored bin (`IGN`), an illegal
  bin (`ILL`, with the rule that reports a hit, or none when a checker owns
  it), a cross exclusion by pattern (`XIGN`, `XILL`) or a tag (`TAG`). Plan
  items are not in the tables (a table can serve several groups): the
  service's declaration names them with `plan()` (Plan Traceability).
* `make build COV=1` builds into `build/<build>-cov/` with code-coverage
  instrumentation of `RTL/` and the AXI4 monitors bound to every
  `AOU_CORE_TOP`. The bench covergroups always sample, but `fc.json` is written
  only with `COV=1`, so a report never silently lacks the monitors' bins.
* Sampling: the L1 monitor per flit; the L2 agent on every state change; L3 on
  every message packed or decoded, every flit built and every credit event;
  the AXI monitor per completed transaction; the T1 checkers at the events they
  judge; the AXI4 monitors one cycle after each channel handshake.
* Each `COV=1` run writes `<run>/fc.json` (every group, coverpoint and cross,
  every bin with its count, zero-hit bins included, samples outside every bin,
  tags, illegal marks and plan items) and `aou_axi4_fc.<run-id>.txt` (its
  `PLAN` lines carry the monitors' plan items).
  `scripts/fc_report.py` (`--help`) union-merges the passing runs of a build
  into `summary.txt` (hit/total per group and point over the headline bins, and
  the `rare`, `v0.8` and `v0.9draft` totals apart),
  `holes.txt` (every unhit bin, tagged ones marked), `index.html` and
  `fc_merged.json`, and lists any illegal bin a passing run hit.
  `make fc_report` is a shorthand; `make cov_report COV=1` adds code coverage,
  merging the register bench's passing runs of the same configuration (VCS maps
  them onto `AOU_CORE_TOP`).
* Verilator and the commercial simulators count code-coverage points
  differently; compare uncovered items per module, not headline percentages.

==== Code-Coverage Exclusions

* The integrator-replaced library cells (`AOU_SOC_SYNCHSR`, `AOU_SOC_GFMUX_LVT`,
  `AOU_SOC_BUF`; integration guide 8.7) are excluded with their reasons in
  `cov/config/vcs/cov_exclude.hier`; reset-only FSM transitions through
  `cov/config/vcs/cov_fsm_reset.filter`. Dead line, branch and FSM items are
  excluded one by one, each with its reason, in `cov/config/vcs/cov_exclude.el`.
* The bring-up controller's L1 to Reset transition is excluded there: the UCIe
  Adapter leaves L1 only through Retrain (UCIe r1.1 8.3.3; only L2 exits
  through Reset), so `pl_state_sts` never goes from L1 straight to Reset. Its
  two transitions into the link-down state, from L1 and from waiting for the
  remote die, stay holes until the UCIe and MAS rules on `pl_clk_req` ordering
  are reviewed.
* The `v0.8` bins (the ERROR state, `cg_flit_occ` half-flit Misc limit) need an
  AoU v0.8 DUT or partner; the four-granule ActivateReq bins lost the tag when
  the DUT started sending it (profile exchange, `cg_act_profile`).
* Verilator scores covergroups as zero; its code coverage (line, branch,
  expression, toggle) is reported by module (`summary.txt`, lcov
  `merged.info`), and the functional numbers come from the service on every
  simulator.
* Toggle coverage is collected and reviewed but is not a closure gate.
