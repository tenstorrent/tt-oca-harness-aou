# Checker inventory

Generated by `scripts/rule_inventory.py` (`make rule_inventory`) from `checkers/rules.csv` and the rule ledgers of the runs under `out/`; edit the CSV, not this file. The normative lists are the AoU specification's 45 checkable rules (v0.7 is the default; rules that exist only in v0.8 are marked) and the 41 FDI handshake rules of UCIe r1.1 chapter 8. Rule texts are summaries; the reference column of the CSV gives the section.

Scope: **DUT** rules bind the DUT and are checked on its outputs. **MODEL** rules bind the partner the testbench models (the Adapter or the remote die, our L1/L2/L3 models): they are self-checked or held by construction. **N/A** rules cannot be exercised through this DUT's interface. A checker written `via AOU-Rn` reports under that rule's ID.

Evidence: *checks* counts the rule's evaluations by its primary checker in passing clean T1 runs (hits on the DUT); *fired* lists runs where it reported a violation: a *negative* test that expected it (injection, including the L3 unit bench), an *escape* run (a re-introduced main bug or an RTL mutation, `make escape`) or a *failing* run. Status *fired* means fired in a negative, escape or mutation run; *hit* means only evaluated in passing runs.

Runs with a ledger: 708.
