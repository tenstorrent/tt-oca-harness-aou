[[aou-dv-verification-plan]]
== AoU Verification Plan

[[aou-vplan-summary]]
=== Executive Summary

*What is verified.* `AOU_TOP`, the AXI-over-UCIe bridge in `RTL/`, as one die
of a two-die link: AXI4 traffic from one to four resource planes (RPs),
carried as AoU messages in 256-byte Format 6 flits over the UCIe Flit-Die
Interface (FDI), and back. The plan has 13 feature areas
(<<aou-vplan-areas>>):

* FDI bring-up and link events.
* Link activation and deactivation.
* Credits and flow control; flit packing and the message format.
* AXI reads and writes: sizes, bursts, responses, ordering, outstanding limits,
  exclusives and data integrity.
* Several RPs at once: DEST_RP routing and QoS arbitration.
* Width conversion between dies of different RP widths (all nine pairings of
  256, 512 and 1024 bits), transaction splitting and aggregation.
* Early write response and the write watchdog.
* Error detection, the debug FIFO and interrupts; the APB register file.
* TX low-power mode; resets, clock ratios, DFT scan mode and the RTL's own
  assertions; throughput and latency.

Eleven build configurations span one to four RPs, 32-, 64- and 128-byte FDIs,
single- and two-PHY builds, non-default RX FIFO depths and the registered
packer output (README "Build configurations").

*Against what.* AoU specification v0.8 decides behaviour; UCIe r1.1 chapter 8
defines the FDI; AMBA AXI4 and APB define the buses. The MAS, the integration
guide (`DOC/integration_guide/`) and the register description
(`csr/aou-core.rdl`) define the implementation's own features: registers,
interrupts, debug, QoS and the watchdog. Expected values never come from the
RTL; where no document defines a behaviour, the bench takes it from the RTL
and says so. Stimulus the specifications leave undefined (illegal AXI,
protocol-illegal FDI sequences, credit overruns) is not run.

*How.* One UVM testbench drives the DUT's AXI and APB ports and models the
remote die in layers (the UCIe Adapter at the FDI, its link-state machine,
the remote AoU protocol layer and its AXI side). A byte-level scoreboard checks
every transaction end to end; always-on checkers report every violation under
a rule traced to a specification section; negative tests declare the errors
they cause and are judged as strictly as any other test. Functional coverage is
counted by the bench over the passing runs of every configuration, code
coverage by the simulator, and throughput is gated against a model of the AoU
packing limit (<<aou-vplan-how>>).

*Where it stands.* Status is generated, never kept by hand: each full
regression writes the plan's status page and its coverage report
(<<aou-vplan-status>>). An item reads "covered" when a passing run of a
scenario that lists it exists, and "covered, with known failures" when some of
its runs fail on a filed DUT issue, naming the configurations.

*Main open items* (each a filed issue or an open pull request):

. *TX throughput* (tenstorrent/tt-oca-harness-aou#131). With several RPs, or
  narrow RPs on a wide FDI, the DUT's TX link reaches 58-87% of the packing
  limit; the throughput windows on `1x256-sp32`, `2xmix-tp64_128` and
  `4xmix4211-sp128` fail on it. Several RPs of mixed width on a 128-byte FDI is
  the main use case, so this is the most important open item.
. *Requests in an ActivateAck's flit* (tenstorrent/tt-oca-harness-aou#127).
  Under the v0.9 draft flit rules (`+v0p9draft`) the DUT sends requests it held
  during activation in its ActivateAck's flit.
. *WLAST-generator assertions* (tenstorrent/tt-oca-harness-aou#132). On the
  assertion builds one RTL assertion stops a legal simulation when AWREADY is
  held low for more than 500 cycles.
. *Outstanding counters* (tenstorrent/tt-oca-harness-aou#136). The DUT's
  outstanding write and read counters wrap at 1024.
. *Dies with different RP counts.* The mapping between them is
  implementation-defined; pull request #124 proposes one.

[[aou-vplan-coverage-closure]]
*Coverage closure (deferred, to follow up).* Spec behaviours with no bin, or
holes that point at a real gap (<<aou-fcov-gaps>> has the detail):

. *DUT-initiated L1 entry* is never exercised: every L1 entry in the bench is
  the Adapter model's.
. *ReadData size against its ReadReq.* Nothing crosses a ReadReq's size with
  the DLENGTH of the ReadData that answers it (an AoU v0.8 rule).
. *Traffic in DEACTIVATE.* No bins for responses sent after a side's own
  DeactivateReq, for who deactivated first, or for activation order.
. *ERROR entered from DISABLED* (an inconsistent request or acknowledge) has no
  bin.
. *Dense WriteResp flits.* Flits with 16 or more WriteResps from the DUT never
  occur; that is the DUT packer's choice.
. *Link events with traffic in flight.* No bin combines a link event (Retrain,
  LinkError, L1, reset) with traffic in flight or the activation state; only
  directed tests cover it.
. *AoU v0.8 profiles and FLEX/USER fields* have no bins; they need a v0.8 DUT.
. *Credit encodings.* No bin records which credit encodings each class sent or
  received (plan item 3.3).

Also carried from the coverage-closure review of 2026-10-14 (recheck each
before starting): functional groups for the features that have none
(registers, interrupts, QoS arbitration outcomes, DEST_RP remapping, the DUT's
own splitter and aggregator, resets during traffic, low-power mode);
cross-layer groups (link event × activation state × outstanding; reset ×
state; zero credit × FDI back-pressure; deactivation phase × message class); a
full DEST_RP permutation sweep; random-traffic knobs for maximum burst length,
maximum ID and a low address window per RP; and a toggle-coverage triage.

[[aou-vplan-scope]]
=== Scope and References

*Summary.* The DUT is `AOU_TOP` with its FDI bring-up controller; everything
around it is modelled. AoU v0.8 decides; the implementation documents decide
what the specification leaves to the implementation.

*In scope:* the 13 areas of <<aou-vplan-areas>>, on the build configurations
of `aou_sim_cfg.toml` (README "Build configurations"; `make configs`).

*Not in scope:*

* The UCIe D2D adapter and PHY below the FDI, which the bench models.
* The SoC fabric behind the AXI ports.
* `AOU_CORE_TOP` integrated without the bring-up controller.
* Stimulus the specifications leave undefined: illegal AXI (a burst across
  4 KB, FIXED or WRAP bursts, which AoU cannot carry), protocol-illegal FDI
  sequences, sending without credit. Behaviour after an error is checked only
  where a document defines it.

*References and the revision rule.*

* AoU specification v0.8 is the deciding reference. The RTL implements the
  v0.7 wire format; the bench checks that format and treats v0.8 features the
  RTL lacks as gaps for the owners, not as DUT failures.
* UCIe r1.1 chapter 8 (FDI), AMBA AXI4, AMBA APB.
* The MAS (`DOC/MAS/`), the integration guide and `csr/aou-core.rdl` for
  registers, interrupts, debug, QoS, the watchdog and other
  implementation-defined behaviour. The RDL, the MAS and the RTL must agree on
  every register and field; a disagreement is reported.
* Where none of these defines a behaviour, the bench's expectation is taken
  from the RTL and labelled so.
* Licensed standards are cited by document and section, never quoted.

[[aou-vplan-how]]
=== How It Is Verified

*Summary.* Stimulus comes from scenario classes and plusargs over one
environment; checks live in always-on checkers; an item's evidence is a passing
run with the checkers active, or a coverage bin when the item is a stimulus
shape; status and coverage are generated from each regression.

==== Stimulus

A T1 test brings the FDI link up through the Adapter model, activates the AoU
link from the side and in the order the scenario chooses, programs the DUT
over APB and drives traffic from the local AXI managers and the remote die's
models. Scenarios compose the base sequence's primitives (link up, activate,
program, traffic, hold, inject, reset, deactivate) and select variants with
plusargs; seeds randomise initiator, order, addresses, sizes, strobes,
attributes, timing, packer layout and credit return policy. The bench and its
layers are described in `AOU_TB_ARCH.adoc`, which also has the naming rules
for scenarios and rule IDs.

==== Checking and Evidence

The scoreboard compares every transaction against golden memories, requires
exactly one response per request, in order within an ID, and nothing
outstanding at the end. The protocol checkers (`AOU_FDI_CHECKER`, the L3 rule
checker, the L1 monitor, the AXI and APB interface checks) and the T1 checkers
(activation, credits, debug FIFOs, early-response back-pressure, software
reset) report every violation under a rule ID from
`uvm/env/checkers/rules.csv`; `docs/checker_inventory.md` lists each rule with
the evidence from the runs.

A plan item is one of two kinds, and its evidence follows:

* *Behaviour* (the DUT must do something right): a passing run of a scenario
  that reaches it, with the checker that judges it active.
* *Stimulus shape* (something must have happened: a size, a burst length, a
  credit encoding, a granule position): a coverage bin. Each family of shapes
  is one item naming its bins (`AOU_FCOV.adoc`, Plan Traceability); a family
  no bin can evidence says so in its note.

==== Coverage

Functional coverage is counted by the bench's coverage service over the
passing runs of every configuration (`AOU_FCOV.adoc`). The closure target is
100% of the headline bins; an unreachable bin is reviewed and then ignored with
its reason, never silently waived. Bins tagged with a spec revision the RTL
doesn't implement (`v0.8`, and `v0.9draft` for a draft flit rule) are left out
of the headline figure and reported apart until the DUT targets that revision.
The AoU v0.8 rule set itself runs between two L3 models on the L3 unit bench
(`+L3_SPEC=v0.8`, group `v0p8` of `testlists/l3_unit.toml`), since the DUT
can't speak v0.8. Code coverage of `RTL/` (line, condition, branch, FSM;
toggle reviewed, not a gate) comes from the simulator, with the
integrator-replaced cells excluded (`docs/DECISIONS.md` D10).

==== Known Failures and Verdicts

`scripts/check_run.py` gives every run a verdict: PASS (no errors), XFAIL
(every error matches an `expected_fail.yaml` entry naming a filed issue), FAIL
(anything else) or XPASS (an entry stopped matching: review it, the bug may be
fixed). The file gets no new entries: a new failure is triaged and reported,
and existing entries only shrink as bugs are fixed. A DUT finding is listed in
this plan once it is a GitHub issue.

| `functional` | Every area but `stress`, `matrix`, `perf` and `assertions`

[[aou-vplan-status]]
==== Status Reporting

The plan carries no status table. Each full regression writes
`out/regress/<RID>/summary.md` (runs and verdicts per build, every FAIL with
the command that repeats it) and `out/regress/<RID>/plan_status.md`, the
status page of this plan for that regression's runs
(`scripts/plan_status.py --rid <RID>`; `make plan_status` for every run under
`out/`). With coverage, the union report is in `out/regress/<RID>/union/`
(`fc/summary.txt`, `fc/holes.txt`, `fc/index.html`).

The status page counts items per feature area. Each item is:

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
| Status | Meaning
| covered | Passing runs of the scenarios that list it, and no other verdict
| covered, with known failures | Passing runs, and XFAIL runs on the configurations its evidence names, each tied to a filed issue
| covered, with failures | Passing runs, and FAIL runs on the configurations named
| xfail-only | No passing run; XFAIL runs only
| failing | No passing run; at least one FAIL
| blocked | Every scenario that lists it is tagged `blocked`
| not covered | No scenario lists it, or none of its scenarios has a judged run
|===

Each row's evidence names the scenarios, configurations and issues behind its
status, and the regression summary has every failing run; that is the
drill-down. Merged items report through their surviving ID; dropped items are
listed with their reason and not counted. Given the functional coverage
(`--fc`, the union's `fc_merged.json` in a coverage regression), the page
also lists each stimulus-shape item with the bins that name it, hit over
total, and its holes; the bins don't change an item's status.

=== Feature Areas

*Summary.* The plan items are grouped into 13 areas
(`docs/plan_areas.yaml`). Item IDs keep the `N.M` numbering of the plan they
came from, so the IDs in an area need not be consecutive. Each area opens with
what is required and how it is evidenced; its item table (generated) follows,
with the scenarios that cite each item.

[[aou-vplan-area-fdi]]
==== FDI Bring-up and Link Events

UCIe r1.1 chapter 8: the FDI handshakes at every FDI width, flit cancel, the
stall handshake, the link states (Retrain, LinkError, L1, LinkReset,
Disabled, link down) and the DUT's bring-up controller (integration guide). The
remote die's Adapter model drives every state change and abort point; the FDI
checker and the L1 monitor judge the DUT's side, and the end-to-end data checks
show that flits survive cancels and stalls. Evidence for the shapes:
`cg_fdi_xfer`, `cg_fdi_cancel`, `cg_fdi_stall`, `cg_link_state`,
`cg_bringup_fsm`. Lists: `fdi`, `g1b`, `lifecycle`.

[[aou-vplan-limitations]]
=== Known Limitations

*Summary.* What the bench does not verify on this RTL, grouped by reason. The
item tables mark each such item `dropped` with its reason.

* *Not in this RTL.* No FDID field (6.16, 9.7); no RX incomplete-flit time-out:
  the Adapter completes or cancels every flit (9.8); `AOU_CORE_TOP` is not
  integrated without the bring-up controller (22.5); the peer's maximum data
  width is fixed at 1024 bits (14.17).
* *INCR bursts only.* AoU carries no AxBURST, so FIXED and WRAP are out of
  scope (4.16, 4.17, 5.16, 5.17); AXI-R19 flags any other burst as a stimulus
  error.
* *Undefined stimulus is not run.* A remote credit overrun (9.5), ReadData
  wider than ARSIZE (9.21) and an asymmetric DEST_RP (12.13) have no defined
  reaction. For malformed messages (1.13, 9.3, 9.4, 9.15, 9.16) the bench
  checks only that it reports them, after which software recovers.
* *PropertyReq.* It has no defined effect in v0.7 and is RsvdZero in v0.8: the
  bench sends PropertyReq 0 only (1.4 dropped, 2.10).
* *Two-PHY builds.* The remote die uses one PHY per run (`+active_phy`), and
  `AOU_FDI_CHECKER` watches that PHY only.
* *FDI state encoding.* The Adapter model uses the UCIe state codes (UCIe r1.1
  Table 8-2) only.
* *Simulators.* VCS and Verilator; covergroups are counted by the coverage
  service, not by Verilator. Xcelium and Questa paths exist and are untested.
* *Spec revisions.* AoU v0.8 features the RTL lacks are checked between models
  only (the `v0p8` group); their bins are reported apart from the headline

=== Appendix D: Plan-Item Provenance

`docs/test_plan_items.yaml` is generated by `scripts/gen_plan_items.py` from an
older test plan (its sections 2.1 to 2.22; item `N.M` was in its section `2.N`)
and `docs/test_plan_corrections.yaml`, which rewords items, merges duplicates
and per-value families into a surviving ID (`merged_into`), drops items not
testable on this RTL (`dropped`, with the reason) and adds items the old plan
lacked (`added`, with the source); every correction has a note. The
`old_test` and `old_status` fields record the old plan's test names and status
column for provenance only; they are never rendered and say nothing about this
bench. Scenarios list the items they cover in `testlists/plan_ids.yaml`;
`scripts/check_testlists.py` checks every T1 and register scenario has an entry
(empty when a scenario covers no item on purpose: the bring-up gate
`aou_sanity_test` and the model self-tests).
